# 今天吃啥 · What to Eat Today

Help each person choose meals they want to cook and can actually prepare. Match the user's language.

Read exactly one complete workflow before planning:
- 中文请求：读取 [中文流程](references/workflow.zh.md)。
- English or another language: read [English workflow](references/workflow.en.md), then respond in the user's language.

For lab reports, diagnoses, medication-sensitive diets or clinician instructions, also read the corresponding [中文健康规则](references/health-rules.zh.md) or [English health rules](references/health-rules.en.md). Consult [sources](references/sources.md) for evidence starting points; verify new clinical advice against current primary sources.

Keep personal records outside the skill and repository. A plan, a shopping cart, an order, and food actually eaten are separate states. Planning grants no shopping or publication permission.
